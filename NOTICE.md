## Adaptation

This package describes and automates an unofficial audio-only adaptation. The
adaptation removes the video stream and copies the original AAC audio packets
without re-encoding. Episode descriptions add links to the corresponding
Stanford HTML and PDF transcripts.

endorsement. “Stanford” and associated marks remain the property of Stanford
University.
